Field Programme Ongoing • 2025–

Climate Clubs in Schools — Katsina State

GNEP is establishing Climate Clubs in schools across Katsina State to embed environmental awareness and climate literacy among young people. This effort creates structured platforms for students to engage with climate science, sustainability, and environmental action.

More detail on reach and outcomes will be shared as the programme is documented.

  • Katsina State, Nigeria
  • Target: Schools and youth
Read Youth-Led Action Article Photo credit: GNEP / AYINET

Climate Literacy Training Programme

GNEP runs a Climate Literacy Training Programme focused on climate and clean-energy awareness for communities in Katsina State. This programme is ongoing; results will be documented and reported as they are verified.
