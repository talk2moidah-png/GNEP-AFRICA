Privacy & Data Protection Policy

How GNEP Africa handles partnership inquiries, research correspondence, and visitor privacy.

Effective Date: October 2026 • Organization: Global Nature and Environmental Partnership (GNEP Africa), Katsina State, Nigeria

1. Our Commitment to Data Privacy

Global Nature and Environmental Partnership (GNEP Africa) respects the privacy of researchers, civil society partners, students, policymakers, and community members who interact with our website and programmes. We collect only the minimum personal information necessary to respond to your direct inquiries and research collaborations.

2. Information You Voluntarily Provide

When you contact us through our Partnership & Inquiry Form or email us directly at gnep.partnership@gmail.com, you may provide your name, organisation, country, email address, and message content. This information is used exclusively to review and respond to your inquiry or partnership request.

3. No Commercial Sale or Third-Party Sharing

GNEP Africa does not sell, rent, trade, or distribute personal contact details to third parties or commercial marketers. Any personal data shared with GNEP is accessible only to authorized GNEP coordination staff responding to your communication.

4. Field Photography & Community Attribution

Field photographs published on this site document verified GNEP and partner activities (including joint restoration drives with AYINET and capacity workshops with The Green Minds Space). If you appear in a documented event photograph and wish to request an update or removal, please contact us directly.

5. Your Rights & Contact Route

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any correspondence records held by GNEP Africa at any time by writing to gnep.partnership@gmail.com or calling +234 706 588 5070.
