Institutional Status & Structure

1. Organisational Governance & Independence

Global Nature and Environmental Partnership (GNEP Africa) was founded in January 2025 as an independent, youth-led environmental research, policy, and community action initiative rooted in Katsina State, Nigeria, with a pan-African outlook.

Leadership & Coordination: Executive coordination is led by Mohammed Labaran Sani (Founder), alongside Project Lead Muhammad Garba Isah, Volunteer Abdulrahman Ibrahim Sabiu, and Branding/Consultancy partner Pandora Innovations.

Honest Disclosure of Governance Evolution: GNEP Africa currently operates under founder and thematic team coordination. Formal board/advisory council charters and external audited financial statements are not claimed until formally constituted and ratified.

Partnership Classification Vocabulary

GNEP strictly distinguishes formal partners from event conveners and prospective collaborators:

  • 1. Implementation Partner: Jointly executes field drives with GNEP (e.g., African Youth Initiative Network — AYINET on the 150 economic tree seedlings drive).
  • 2. Workshop Co-Host: Co-designs and co-delivers capacity trainings with GNEP (e.g., The Green Minds Space on the 20-CSO Policy Brief Workshop).
  • 3. Event Participant (Not Organiser): Institutional events where GNEP attended as a participant (e.g., SUBEB Katsina & UNICEF workshop on 19 August 2026).
  • 4. Independent Data / Policy Source: External institutions cited in GNEP research (e.g., NBS, World Bank, NiMet, NEMA, CPI, IPCC) with no implied endorsement.
Editorial & Publication Governance

2. Research Integrity, Authorship & Content Lifecycle

All publications hosted in the GNEP Environmental Knowledge Hub adhere to four editorial safeguards:

Named Authorship

Every resource clearly identifies whether it is an official GNEP institutional output, a collaborative partner report, or an independent author analysis hosted for policy dialogue.

Primary Source Traceability

Empirical claims cite specific primary sources, publication dates, and methodological caveats (e.g., distinguishing modelled mortality projections from recorded tolls).

Corrections & Versioning

Material factual updates or date corrections are explicitly logged inside the publication text rather than silently overwritten.

Controlled Status Labels

All records carry a controlled status: Draft, Under Review, Published, Updated, or Archived.

Community & Youth Protection

3. Safeguarding & Field Code of Conduct

Because GNEP works directly with secondary schools (Climate Clubs), youth volunteers, and rural communities in Katsina State, all field activities follow strict safeguarding principles:

  • School & Youth Safety: All school-based Climate Club sessions are conducted with the authorisation and presence of school educators and administrators.
  • Ethical Photography & Attribution: Field photographs document actual activities and credit partner organisations accurately.
  • Non-Exploitation: Community and youth participation is voluntary, educational, and non-discriminatory.
