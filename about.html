An African Environmental
& Research Partnership

Founded in January 2025 in Katsina State, Nigeria, the Global Nature and Environmental Partnership (GNEP Africa) bridges the persistent divide between high-level African climate policy discussions and community-level realities.

Our vision is a resilient and sustainable Africa where people and nature thrive together—delivered across three interconnected pillars: Community and Schools, Civil Society and Institutions, and Research and Policy.

Led by Dedicated Youth Researchers & Practitioners

Guided by Founder Mohammed Labaran Sani alongside Project Lead Muhammad Garba Isah, Volunteer Abdulrahman Ibrahim Sabiu, and Branding/Consultancy partner Pandora Innovations, GNEP Africa combines empirical academic rigor with verified field action.

Every claim on our platform is governed by strict research integrity standards, distinguishing direct outputs from long-term ecological outcomes and crediting implementation partners such as AYINET and The Green Minds Space.

Institutional Service Division

GNEP Consultancy & Capacity Development

Alongside our grassroots environmental restoration and open-access research repository, GNEP operates a dedicated Consultancy & Capacity Development division.

We partner with primary and secondary schools, universities, government ministries, NGOs, international development agencies, and individual learners to deliver practical AI literacy training, youth & student AI education, policy research and brief-writing training, AI-powered digital innovation, and climate policy consultancy.
