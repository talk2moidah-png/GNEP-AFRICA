Accessibility Statement

Ensuring our environmental research, policy briefs, and field documentation are accessible to all visitors.

Target Standard: Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA

1. Accessibility Measures Implemented

  • Keyboard Navigation & Skip Links: Every page includes a visible-on-focus Skip to main content link, logical tab order, and high-visibility focus rings (`#0284C7`).
  • Colour Contrast: Text, buttons, badges, and navigation dropdown states are engineered to exceed WCAG 2.2 AA contrast thresholds (minimum 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text/UI components).
  • Semantic Structure: Pages use semantic HTML5 landmarks (`header`, `nav`, `main`, `section`, `article`, `aside`, `footer`), one clear `H1` per page, and associated form labels.
  • Motion Sensitivity: Animations, counters, and 3D canvas effects automatically respect your operating system's prefers-reduced-motion: reduce preference.
  • Linguistic Accessibility: Recognising that climate communication must reach frontline communities, GNEP publishes public climate advisories in both English and Hausa.

2. Feedback & Assistance

If you encounter any accessibility barrier on our website or require a publication in an alternative accessible format, please contact us at gnep.partnership@gmail.com or +234 706 588 5070.
