Methodological Transparency

4. Data Provenance, Licensing & Scope Limitations

1. No Implied Real-Time Telemetry

This platform synthesizes periodic published datasets (monthly NBS Price Watch, seasonal NiMet/WMO advisories, annual CPI/World Bank updates, and GNEP project logs). It does not stream live sensor telemetry.

2. Observation vs. Projection

Epidemiological heat-mortality projections (such as Climate Impact Lab's 31,400 figure for Sep 2026–Feb 2027) and seasonal rainfall outlooks represent modelled risk scenarios above a historical baseline, not recorded administrative tallies.

3. Community Privacy & Coordinates

GNEP field markers are plotted at the Local Government Area or town centroid level (e.g., Katsina metropolis `12.99° N, 7.61° E`) to protect school and household privacy.
