Explore the Programme

Programme Overview

Education for All is a GNEP flagship initiative designed to widen access to meaningful learning, strengthen foundational and digital capabilities, build climate and AI literacy, support young people and underserved communities, and connect education with mentorship, entrepreneurship and pathways to decent work.

Why Education for All

Education inequality, digital exclusion, and climate knowledge gaps limit opportunities for millions of children and young people in Africa. This programme addresses those barriers by delivering inclusive, future‑oriented learning.

Mission, Vision & Belief

Mission: To expand access to inclusive and relevant learning by mobilising educators, volunteers, institutions and communities.

Vision: A society where children, young people and underserved communities can access quality learning, acquire relevant knowledge and skills, and pursue dignified livelihoods.

Guiding belief: Empowerment is strengthened when knowledge, opportunity and practical support are shared.

Skills Acquisition

Vocational training, entrepreneurship, financial literacy and workplace readiness.

Youth Entrepreneurship

Idea generation, business planning, digital entrepreneurship and social impact.

Mentor networks, youth leadership, civic participation and peacebuilding.

Employment Pathways

Career guidance, internships, apprenticeships and employer engagement.

MID‑EYE Framework
