Five-Stage Results-Chain Governance

How GNEP Africa distinguishes direct activity outputs from long-term ecological outcomes

Results Chain Stage Institutional Definition GNEP Africa Verified Example Verification Standard
1. Inputs Human, technical, partner, and material resources committed to an intervention. Facilitator expertise, nursery seedlings, training manuals, co-host venue with AYINET & The Green Minds Space. Internal programme records & partner agreements.
2. Activities Actions and field operations directly executed by GNEP or co-hosts. Conducting field planting drives, convening CSO policy brief drafting sessions, publishing policy analyses. Dated event logs & authenticated field photography.
3. Outputs Direct, countable products and immediate deliverables of an activity. 150 economic tree seedlings planted; 20 CSO representatives trained; 17 publications released. Attendance sheets, seedling counts, and repository codes.
4. Outcomes Medium-term changes in participant behaviour, institutional practice, or seedling survival. Trained CSOs submitting evidence-based briefs to state ministries; planted seedlings surviving dry-season stress. Follow-up surveys and 90-day / annual field monitoring.
5. Long-Term Impact Durable ecological restoration, climate resilience, or statutory policy compliance. Micro-climate stabilization, improved sub-national budget transparency, and reduced community vulnerability. Multi-year longitudinal data and independent counterfactuals.

About GNEP Africa

Global Nature and Environmental Partnership (GNEP Africa) — Founded in January 2025 in Katsina State, Nigeria.

We empower people and communities with the knowledge, partnerships, and opportunities needed to respond to climate and environmental challenges and build a resilient, sustainable Africa where people and nature thrive together.
