Terms of Use & Attribution Standards

Guidelines for citing GNEP Africa research, policy briefs, and field documentation.

Last Updated: November 2026 • Scope: GNEP Africa Website & Environmental Knowledge Hub

1. Purpose of the GNEP Africa Platform

This website and the GNEP Environmental Knowledge Hub are operated by Global Nature and Environmental Partnership (GNEP Africa) to share verified field updates, environmental policy briefs, climate literacy resources, and partnership pathways.

2. Open Knowledge & Citation Requirements

Unless otherwise stated on an individual publication, GNEP-authored policy briefs, working papers, and educational resources (including Hausa-language climate advisories) may be downloaded, shared, and cited for non-commercial educational, academic, civil society, and policymaking purposes, provided full attribution is given to the author(s) and GNEP Africa using the suggested APA or Chicago citation provided on each publication page.

3. Independent Policy Analyses & Disclaimers

Independent policy briefs hosted in the Knowledge Hub reflect the evidence-informed analysis of their named authors and cited primary datasets (such as the National Bureau of Statistics, World Bank, NiMet, NEMA, and Climate Policy Initiative). They are provided for public policy dialogue and academic scrutiny.

4. Photography & Partner Credits

Photographs in the Field Documentation Gallery and article pages are copyrighted by GNEP Africa and credited partners (including AYINET, The Green Minds Space, and cited documentary photographers). They may not be repurposed for misleading claims or unverified fundraising.
